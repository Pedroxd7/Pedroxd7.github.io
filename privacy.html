Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clipador is a personal-use desktop tool created and operated by Lucas Albino ("I"). It helps a creator turn highlights of livestreams into short vertical videos.

What the app does with data

  • The app runs on the user's own computer. Videos are downloaded, analyzed and edited locally. I do not operate any server that receives your videos or personal data.
  • If the user connects a TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the display name). They are used only to send videos that the user has reviewed and approved to the user's own TikTok account.
  • The access token is stored only on the user's computer, and I do not have access to it.
  • If the user chooses to configure an optional AI service key, short text transcripts of the selected clips may be sent to that AI provider (Anthropic) to rate and title them. This is off unless the user sets a key.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties.

Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions) and delete the app's local files from your computer.
